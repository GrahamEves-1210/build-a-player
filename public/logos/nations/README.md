# Nation flags (`<FIFA code>.png`, 500 x 500)

- The 116 first ones (the 2026 World Cup's 48, Career's 68 more, Russia): ESPN, `a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/countries/500`.
- The other 94 (added 2026-11-25 so every FIFA member is in Career): flagcdn.com (Flagpedia.net), `flagcdn.com/w640/<iso>.png`.
  National flags are public domain; Flagpedia's images are free to use. Each one is drawn the way the ESPN files are:
  the flag in its own proportions 442 px wide, in a 9 px #2B2C2D frame, centred on a transparent 500 x 500 canvas
  (Nepal, not a rectangle, as it is with no frame). Tahiti is French Polynesia's flag (`pf`); Chinese Taipei is `tw`.
